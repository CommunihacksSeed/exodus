# Human review sheet

Generated by `npm run review-sheet`. Do not edit by hand.

Software has confirmed that every quote below appears word for word in its source. It cannot
tell whether a sentence says more than its quote supports. That is what this review is for.

For each sentence, ask:

1. Does the quote support everything the sentence says, including numbers and dates?
2. Is the right body named? Staff advise, committees recommend, only Council decides.
3. Has a condition been lost ("subject to", "and/or", "up to")?
4. Would a resident act wrongly after reading it?

To sign off an item, set `review.humanReviewedBy` and `review.reviewedOn` (YYYY-MM-DD) in its
file under `data/explanations/`, then run `npm run data`. The page then shows who checked it.

## 2027.PB1.6: A taller base for the new affordable housing building at 1337 Queen Street West

- Official title: 1337 Queen Street West - Demolition and New Construction on a Designated Heritage Property under Part V, Section 42 of the Ontario Heritage Act - Approval
- City page: https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2027.PB1.6
- Location shown: 1337 Queen Street West (City of Toronto geocoder (map.toronto.ca), address match for "1337 Queen St W", retrieved 2026-11-12)

### What is being proposed?

- [ ] **City staff recommend that City Council allow the existing building at 1337 Queen Street West to be torn down and a new 16-storey building to be built in its place.**
  - > “City Council approve the demolition of the non-contributing building and the erection of a new 16-storey mixed-use building at 1337 Queen Street West”
    — [City of Toronto item page 2027.PB1.6](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2027.PB1.6#:~:text=City%20Council%20approve%20the,1337%20Queen%20Street%20West)
- [ ] **The new building would be a 16-storey tower on a 4-storey base. It would include affordable housing, and the City would keep owning it.**
  - > “a new 16-storey mixed-use tower with a 4-storey podium building that will include purpose-built affordable housing; and which will remain under ownership of the City”
    — [City of Toronto item page 2027.PB1.6](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2027.PB1.6#:~:text=a%20new%2016%2Dstorey%20mixed%2Duse,ownership%20of%20the%20City)
- [ ] **In June 2026 City Council approved a heritage permit for this project. The only design change now is that the 4-storey base would be 1.2 metres taller.**
  - > “A heritage permit for the project was previously approved on June 24 and 25, 2026, by City Council.”
    — [City of Toronto item page 2027.PB1.6](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2027.PB1.6#:~:text=A%20heritage%20permit%20for,2026%2C%20by%20City%20Council.)
  - > “All other details of the scheme remain as those approved by City Council in June 2026.”
    — [Staff report from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, page 4](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2027/pb/bgrd/backgroundfile-290746.pdf#page=4)

### Why?

- [ ] **The base was made taller so the community space on the ground floor can have higher ceilings.**
  - > “The revised proposal increases the height of the 4-storey podium building by 1.2m in order to increase the floor-to-ceiling heights within the ground floor community space.”
    — [Staff report from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, page 4](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2027/pb/bgrd/backgroundfile-290746.pdf#page=4)
- [ ] **With the taller base, the design no longer follows two rules in the Parkdale Main Street heritage district plan. That is why a new heritage approval is needed.**
  - > “It also does not include the 5.0m step back required by the PHCD Plan for any parts of the new building that are taller than the adjacent contributing property.”
    — [City of Toronto item page 2027.PB1.6](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2027.PB1.6#:~:text=It%20also%20does%20not,the%20adjacent%20contributing%20property.)
  - > “Consequently, a new Section 42 approval under the Ontario Heritage Act is required.”
    — [City of Toronto item page 2027.PB1.6](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2027.PB1.6#:~:text=Consequently%2C%20a%20new%20Section,Heritage%20Act%20is%20required.)
- [ ] **The building is part of the City-led Parkdale Hub project, which is meant to add affordable rental housing, better community facilities and updated public spaces.**
  - > “to provide new affordable rental housing, improved community facilities, and modernized public spaces”
    — [Staff report from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, page 3](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2027/pb/bgrd/backgroundfile-290746.pdf#page=3)
- [ ] **Staff's opinion is that the taller base would not harm the heritage character of the area, because this part of Queen Street West does not have a consistent row of building fronts today.**
  - Keep in mind: This is the opinion of City staff. No board or Council has agreed or disagreed with it yet.
  - > “this section of south side of Queen Street West has neither a consistent nor a continuous street wall”
    — [Staff report from the Senior Manager, Heritage Planning, page 1](https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2027/pb/bgrd/backgroundfile-290746.pdf#page=1)
  - > “would not harm the character or appearance of the District or be contrary to the objectives of the PHCD Plan”
    — [City of Toronto item page 2027.PB1.6](https://secure.toronto.ca/council/agenda-item.do?item=2027.PB1.6#:~:text=would%20not%20harm%20the,of%20the%20PHCD%20Plan)
